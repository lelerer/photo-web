8 seconds away from the earth



Earlier in 2024, during my training to become an assitant freedive instructor, I blacked out near the surface after my very first attempt to 32m due to being low on oxygen, mental stress, maybe the thermocline at 30m, and a lot of other factors.....

I was unconscious for about 8 seconds according to my buddy, who rescued me back in time and literally saved my life. I have no memory during that 8 seconds, yet over time this experience is taking on deeper significance for me.......
